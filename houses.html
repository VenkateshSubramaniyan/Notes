Maraka Bhāvas · Classical Jyotiṣa

Why Are the 2nd and 7th Houses Called Maraka Houses?

In Vedic astrology, Maraka literally means “that which can cause an ending.” The 7th house is linked to the primary longevity reference (the 8th house), while the 2nd is linked to the secondary longevity reference (the 3rd house); both are traditionally associated with maraka potential. This is a technical astrological doctrine—not a claim that these houses or their occupants inevitably cause death.

07

7th House · Yuvati Bhāva

12th from the 8th · Primary reference

The 8th house is the principal house associated with longevity, lifespan and major transformations. The 7th is the 12th from the 8th · Primary reference, so it is interpreted as the house of depletion or loss relative to the longevity house. This is the central positional explanation for its maraka role.

The 7th is also opposite the Lagna (the 1st house), which represents the native. In traditional maraka analysis, the 7th house and its lord can therefore receive particular attention—but this symbolism alone does not establish a death prediction.

02

2nd House · Dhana Bhāva

12th from the 3rd · Secondary reference

The 3rd house is commonly treated as the 8th from the 8th and is used as a secondary longevity house. The 2nd is the 12th house counted from the 3rd; the 12th-from relationship signifies loss or depletion of the matters represented by the house it is counted from. This is one traditional rationale for assigning maraka significance to the 2nd.

The 2nd is also a maraka sthāna in classical longevity rules, alongside the 7th. Its ordinary significations—wealth, speech, family and food—remain important and are not inherently harmful.

How maraka potential is assessed

  • 2nd- and 7th-house lords: traditionally examined as maraka lords.
  • Planets placed in the 2nd or 7th: may be considered in maraka analysis depending on the school and chart context.
  • Daśā and antardaśā: timing is traditionally assessed only after evaluating the whole chart and longevity framework.
  • Whole-chart context: the 8th house and its lord, the 3rd house, Saturn, planetary strength, aspects and relevant divisional factors may also be examined.

Important: These are traditional astrological interpretations, not scientifically validated methods for predicting death. A 2nd- or 7th-house placement, a maraka lord, or a particular daśā should not be used on its own to predict when or whether someone will die.
