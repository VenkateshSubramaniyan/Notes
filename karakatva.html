Bhāva Kārakatvas

A practical reference to the principal topics represented by each of the twelve houses in Vedic astrology, with commonly used natural significators (naisargika kārakas).

How to read this page
Bhāva kārakatva means the subjects signified by a house. A natural kāraka is a planet that signifies a topic by its inherent symbolism; the house lord is the ruler of the sign occupying that house in a particular chart. They are related but not interchangeable.

How to Predict Uyir and Jada Kārakatvas

Yes — Uyir can be strong while Jada is weak, and Jada can be strong while Uyir is weak. A house represents multiple subjects, and each subject can have different significators and different influences. Judge the specific subject rather than assigning one strength to the entire house.

Uyir • உயிர்

Living beings and life-related matters

  1. Identify the living subject represented by the house (for example, mother in the 4th or spouse in the 7th).
  2. Examine the relevant natural kāraka.
  3. Assess the house, house lord, occupants, aspects and relevant divisional chart.
  4. Judge the particular relationship or living outcome; do not infer everything from one planet.

Jada • ஜடம்

Non-living, material and tangible matters

  1. Identify the material subject (for example, property or vehicles in the 4th, savings in the 2nd).
  2. Examine the natural kāraka relevant to that asset.
  3. Assess the house lord and other relevant asset or wealth indicators.
  4. Judge acquisition, ownership, stability or accumulation as separate outcomes.

Four possible combinations

Uyir strong · Jada weakPeople-related matters may be better supported than material acquisition or stability.
Jada strong · Uyir weakMaterial outcomes may be supported while relationships or people-related matters face challenges.
Both strongBoth categories have supportive indications, subject to the full chart and timing.
Both weakBoth categories may face challenges, with different causes, expressions and timing.

Worked example: 4th house

FactorUyir — MotherJada — Property
Relevant significatorMoonMars is an important property significator; also consider the 4th lord and other asset indicators.
Hypothetical conditionMoon and maternal indicators are well-supportedMars and property indicators are afflicted or poorly supported
Possible interpretationMaternal care or the relationship may be supportedProperty acquisition or ownership may encounter obstacles

How to assess strength: consider the house and its lord, the natural kāraka, dignity, Shadbala where available, conjunctions, aspects, relevant supporting houses, divisional charts and daśā timing. Exaltation does not guarantee every result, and debilitation does not automatically deny it.

Tradition note: Uyir/Jada is a useful analytical distinction, but there is no single universally standardized classical Jyotiṣa score that measures these categories independently. The examples are illustrative, not fixed predictions.

Important distinction: house topics describe the area of life; the house lord describes how that area functions in a specific horoscope; natural kārakas provide an additional significator layer. No single factor should be read in isolation.
