Kundli Matching Calculator

Enter birth details, or just Rasi and Nakshatra for either partner. With birth details for both, the charts are cast (Lahiri sidereal), then matched at chart level: Ashtakoota, Dasa Porutham, Manglik, 7th house, karakas, Navamsa, Dasha and Sade Sati.

City coordinates come from a built-in list and the timezone is estimated from the coordinates and birth date (daylight saving and historical offsets included, approximate near borders); please verify for old or unusual births. Planet positions use a compact analytical ephemeris (about 0.1° to 0.5° accuracy), Lahiri ayanamsa and whole-sign houses. A birth time off by a few minutes can change the Lagna or Moon pada, so verify borderline degrees with a professional ephemeris. Vashya scoring is simplified. This is guidance, not a substitute for an astrologer’s review.
