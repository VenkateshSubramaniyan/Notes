Sign changes (ingress)

Each step starts at the start time you set (local time of the chosen place, shown on the chart for hourly steps); the end date is reached at the same time of day. Lagna is computed for the chosen place and moves about one sign every two hours. Positions use compact approximate orbital elements. The Moon is accurate to a few arcminutes; other planets to about an arcminute or two. Rahu and Ketu are the mean nodes. Date range: 1800 to 2050 recommended.
