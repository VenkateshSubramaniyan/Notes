Kartari

What Kartari means

Kartari means scissors. Take any house or planet and look at the house before it (12th from it) and the house after it (2nd from it).

  • Papa Kartari: malefics in both flanking houses. Sun, Mars, Saturn, Rahu and Ketu are the usual malefics. Many astrologers also count the functional malefics of that Lagna.
  • Shubha Kartari: benefics (Jupiter, Venus, well-placed Mercury, a bright Moon) in both flanking houses. It protects and lifts the hemmed point.
  • Mixed: a benefic on one side and a malefic on the other. This is not a Kartari, only a mixed influence.

One malefic alone is never a Kartari. Both blades must be present. It is read by sign (rashi) in most texts. Some astrologers also test it by bhava cusp.

The idea is the same for houses and planets: whatever sits in the middle has no free space. The two flanking houses are the “blades”, and the quality of the planets in them decides whether the middle is squeezed or cradled.

Papa Kartari

Definition: malefics in both the 12th and 2nd from the point. The 12th-side planet drains energy and the 2nd-side planet cuts off support, so the middle is boxed in.

  • Who counts as malefic: Sun, Mars, Saturn, Rahu and Ketu. A waning Moon and an afflicted Mercury are sometimes added. Many astrologers also use the functional malefics of that Lagna, such as the 6th, 8th and 12th lords.
  • What it does: pressure, delay and a feeling of limited room in whatever the hemmed house or planet signifies.
  • Where it matters most: the Lagna, the Moon and the Lagna lord first, then the house or planet in question.
  • Not automatic doom: its real strength depends on the factors in the strong and weak section below.

Shubha Kartari

Definition: benefics in both the 12th and 2nd from the point. Jupiter, Venus, a well-placed Mercury and a bright Moon are the usual ones.

Benefics on both sides act like a protective cradle. A house or planet in Shubha Kartari gains support, reputation and steadiness in its significations. On the Lagna it supports health and self-confidence. On the Moon it brings calm and emotional security. On a weak house it will not cure the weakness, but it softens it. Its strength rises when the flanking benefics are strong in sign and the house lord is well placed.

Mixed case: one benefic and one malefic is not a Kartari. It is a mixed influence, and the house simply carries both flavours.

Further elaborations

With the three types clear, the sections below go deeper: where to check it, how it plays out in each house and planet, how strong it is, how long it acts, and what cancels it.

How to check it in a chart

  1. Pick the point: a house, or a planet’s sign.
  2. Find the sign before it and the sign after it.
  3. List who sits in each. Empty means no blade on that side.
  4. Classify each occupant as benefic or malefic for this Lagna, not only by nature.
  5. Both malefic means Papa, both benefic means Shubha, anything else is mixed.
  6. Then run the relief checks: aspects, house lord strength, occupants, degrees.

Natural versus functional: Jupiter rules the 8th and 11th for Taurus and Mars rules the 7th and 12th, so labels shift with the Lagna. Mercury is benefic alone or with benefics, and a waning Moon is weaker than a bright one. Rahu and Ketu act like their dispositor and conjunctions. Always state which method you used.

Strong and weak Papa Kartari

Stronger

  • Falls on the Lagna, the Moon or the Lagna lord.
  • Both flanking malefics are strong: own sign, exalted, or close in degrees.
  • No benefic occupies either flank or aspects the house.
  • The hemmed house lord is weak, debilitated, combust or in a dusthana.
  • The house holds malefics too, or is empty and unsupported.
  • The kartari planets run the dasha, with a transit trigger.

Weaker

  • Falls on an ordinary house, not the Lagna or Moon.
  • Flanking malefics are weak, debilitated, combust or far apart in degrees.
  • Jupiter or Venus aspects the house or either flank.
  • The house lord is strong, in a kendra or trikona.
  • Flanking planets are functional benefics for that Lagna.
  • Benefics sit in the hemmed house.

Will it work through life?

Not constantly. Most traditions treat Kartari as a background condition that switches on at certain times, and not as a lifelong sentence.

  • Dashas: it shows most in the dasha or antardasha of the hemmed house lord, its occupants, and the two flanking planets.
  • Transits: Saturn, Mars or the nodes passing over the hemmed house or the flanking signs can re-trigger it.
  • Early life: on the Lagna or Moon the pressure is usually felt first in childhood and youth, around health, confidence or security, and often eases once the Lagna lord or a benefic dasha takes over.
  • Other houses: the matter itself (marriage, children, money) carries the delay. It does not go away automatically, but the benefic aspects and strong lord changes how it ends.
  • Other charts: check the navamsha (D9) and the divisional chart for that matter (D10 for career, D7 for children). If the pattern repeats there, it is more serious. If not, it is likely diluted.

Treat it as a modifier, not a verdict. One yoga never decides a life by itself.

Cancellations and relief

  1. Benefic in a flanking house. The “both sides” condition fails, so it becomes mixed or Shubha.
  2. Benefic aspect. Jupiter is the most-cited reliever. Venus and a strong Mercury also help. Aspect on the house, on its lord, or on both flanks all count.
  3. Strong house lord. Own sign, exalted, or in a kendra or trikona.
  4. Benefic occupying the hemmed house. It shields the house, though the Kartari still limits its output.
  5. Malefics that behave well. Flanking planets in own sign, exalted, or functional benefics for that Lagna do much less harm. Rahu or Mars in the 3rd, 6th or 11th are comfortable.
  6. Weak flanking malefics. Debilitated, combust, or conjunct a benefic, or very far apart by degree.
  7. Lagna and Moon are free of it. Overall life and mind are then protected even if another house is hemmed.
  8. Remedial practice. Traditional options include worship and mantras for the hemmed house lord and the flanking planets, and service and charity linked to their significations. Treat these as supportive practice, not a guarantee. A qualified astrologer can match them to the full chart.

Classical sources agree on the basic definition but differ on how much weight to give it, which planets count as malefic, and sign versus cusp counting. This page gives the common reading. Interpretation depends on the whole chart.
