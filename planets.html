South Indian Rasi Chart — Permanent (Natural) Subathuvam / Paavathuvam

This chart classifies signs by the natural nature of their ruling graha, not by functional lordship. Jupiter and Venus are generally natural benefics; Sun, Mars and Saturn are natural malefics; Moon and Mercury are variable. Rahu/Ketu classification varies by tradition.
